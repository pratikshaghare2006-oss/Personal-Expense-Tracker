About the project

Personal finance made simple

ExpenseTrack is a student-friendly full-stack web application for managing everyday money.

Our purpose

The application helps users understand income, expenses, budgets and financial summaries through a simple blue interface.

Technology

HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node.js, Express and PostgreSQL provide the foundation for this project.
